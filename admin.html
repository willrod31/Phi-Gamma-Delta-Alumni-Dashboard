How sending works

  • Email goes out from the chapter Google account in batches of 50, with everyone in BCC, so no one sees anyone else's address.
  • Each batch is also addressed to the chapter account, so you get a copy of what was sent.
  • Only addresses in the alumni directory can receive email.
  • Free Gmail accounts can email about 100 recipients a day. For bigger sends, use Copy addresses and paste them into BCC in your own email.
